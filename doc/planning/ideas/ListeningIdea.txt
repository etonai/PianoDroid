The goal for this app is to listen to the microphone and identify the piano chord being played.

The main screen will have a single button "Listen"

On the Listen screen, the microphone will listen for music being played, and will identify the note or notes.
When it identifies a note or chord, it will display the note or chord being played on the screen.
If the app detects noise above ambient level, but does not identify the note or notes, it will just display a large light blue dot.
